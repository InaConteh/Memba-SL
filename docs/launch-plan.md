# Launch plan and monetisation

Full working plan, with scripts, a 30-day timeline and a weekly scoreboard: [Memba — Launch & Go-to-Market Plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/X83yHUPBokwRoEthkiwyXZ) (written 22 Nov 2026).

> **Segment note:** that plan's examples use provision shops. The pilot in `docs/blueprint.md` targets **schools and wholesalers**. The method (visits, free trial, pay at day 14, scoreboard) carries over unchanged. Swap the examples and scripts for the pilot segment before using them.

## Position

Sell it as **"get back what you're owed"**: money recovered, measured in NLe. Not as "an app" and not as a free ledger. Owners pay for money recovered; they don't pay for software.

## Goal

**3 businesses paying by Fri 18 Dec 2026. 25 active and 10 paying by Fri 19 Feb 2027.**

Funnel to reach 3 paying. Every rate is an assumption until pilot data replaces it:

| Step | Assumed rate | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Businesses visited | — | 70 |
| Real conversation with the owner | 60% | 42 |
| 2-minute demo | 50% | 21 |
| Free 2-week trial | 40% | 8 |
| Pays at the end | 40% | 3 |

## Channels, in order

1. **In person:** visits and live demos with the owner's real records.
2. **Warm network:** introductions from people who know owners.
3. **Wholesalers and suppliers:** one sells on credit to many.
4. **Associations:** 5 minutes at a members' meeting, with one real pilot result.
5. **WhatsApp Status, Facebook, TikTok:** waitlist and proof, not sales.
6. **LinkedIn:** credibility with partners and judges, real numbers only.
7. **Radio:** only after there are results to show.
8. **SEO:** last.

## Gates

| Gate | Pass when | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | A new record takes under 5 seconds; one real payment works end to end | Start free trials |
| B | 3 businesses have paid (by Fri 18 Dec) | Paid pilot |
| C | 10 paying, 25 active, launch checklist done | Public launch |

**Switch rule:** if 8 or more trials ran and nobody paid by Fri 18 Dec, change the segment or the positioning for the next 4 weeks.

## Monetisation

**Recommended: a free record book, a paid subscription for reminders, and a small fee on collections later.** Start with the subscription alone.

| Order | Model | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Subscription (core) | Flat fee per month, or weekly by mobile money if cash flow is daily. Price below a tenth of what a business recovers in month 1 (common advice, not researched). Pilot: [PILOT PRICE] with a 30-day full refund |
| 2 | Free tier | Records and manual reminders for up to [N] customers. Automation and SMS stay on paid plans, because SMS costs money |
| 3 | Fee on collections | Once most repayments go through Memba pay links. Say clearly who pays it: the business or the customer |
| 4 | SMS top-ups | Like airtime: covers SMS cost for small businesses |
| 5 | Wholesaler / distributor plans | One account manages many retailers' balances; may sponsor their fees |

**Avoid:** selling or sharing data, late fees on customers, ads in reminders, and lending or credit scoring. Lending could come much later, only with customer consent, a licensed partner and Sierra Leone's data protection law in force. The law is still to be tabled in Parliament as of April 2026 ([source](https://moice.gov.sl/?p=2375)).

**Watch:** subscription price ÷ NLe recovered per business per month.

## Rules that affect messaging

- **WhatsApp opt-in:** automated, business-initiated WhatsApp messages need the customer's opt-in, naming the business. In person or on paper counts ([source](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/getting-opt-in)). Owner-sent messages from their own phone, as in the pilot, are ordinary chats.
- **SMS:** two-way SMS is not supported through messaging platforms in Sierra Leone. Some carriers (Africell) may change the sender name. Recommended sending hours are 8 am to 8 pm, with STOP handling in English and local languages ([source](https://sent.dm/resources/sl_sms_guidance), an SMS provider's guide, not the regulator).
- **Never hold customers' money.** Payments go straight to the business's own wallet through a licensed provider.

This is not legal advice. Check money and data questions with a lawyer or the relevant authority before a public launch.
